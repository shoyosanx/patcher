Tampermonkey Script

Install the browser script to preserve HQ uploads on TikTok

What this does

This Tampermonkey script runs on tiktok.com/tiktokstudio/upload and automatically strips TikTok's re-encode flags, ensuring your patched video stays in its original quality after upload.

If your upload includes a song tag, this script is highly recommended to preserve quality. However, if you do not include a song tag, your video is safe to upload without Tampermonkey.

Installation

  1. Install Tampermonkey (Chrome) or Tampermonkey (Firefox).
  2. Click one of the "Install Script" buttons below.
  3. Tampermonkey will open an install tab. Click Install.
  4. Go to tiktok.com/tiktokstudio/upload and upload your patched video.

⚠️ Important

Please enable the Allow User Scripts extension setting. Click here for more info how to do this.
